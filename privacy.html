1. Scope

This Privacy Policy describes the information Mister Sinister is designed to receive and process when users interact with the bot on Discord.

2. Information Discord Provides

When you use a Discord bot, Discord may provide information necessary for the interaction, such as your Discord user ID, username/display name, server or guild information, channel information, and the command or message you send. The exact information available depends on Discord's systems, permissions, and how the bot is configured.

3. Information Mister Sinister Uses

Mister Sinister may process:

  • Command inputs: text you provide to create a Spider-Sona, Spider-Man character, spider hybrid, spooky quote request, or image prompt.
  • Discord identifiers: identifiers needed to respond to you and operate the bot in a server.
  • Generated results: character descriptions, quotes, images, and related outputs produced from your requests.
  • Operational information: limited technical information needed to prevent abuse, diagnose errors, enforce rate limits, or keep the service functioning.

4. Storage and Retention

Mister Sinister should store only information needed to provide the features that are actually enabled. Temporary command inputs may be retained long enough to complete a request or diagnose a problem. If a future feature allows users to save Spider-Sonas or other characters, saved content may be retained until it is deleted, the feature is removed, or retention is otherwise required for legitimate operational or legal reasons.

We do not intend to retain message history simply for the purpose of building a profile of users.

5. How Information Is Used

Information may be used to operate commands, generate requested content, save explicitly requested creations, prevent abuse, troubleshoot failures, maintain security, and comply with applicable legal obligations.

6. Third-Party Processing

Some features may rely on third-party infrastructure, such as Discord hosting/infrastructure or an image-generation provider. Information required to complete a request may be transmitted to those services. Their own privacy policies and terms may also apply.

We will not intentionally sell personal information to advertisers.

7. User Choices and Deletion

If Mister Sinister stores a user's saved character or other user-provided content, the bot should provide a way to request deletion when that feature is implemented. You may also stop using the bot or ask the owner to remove stored information through the contact method listed on this page.

Some information may need to be retained when required for security, fraud prevention, legal compliance, dispute resolution, or other legitimate operational purposes.

8. Children's Privacy

Mister Sinister is a Discord application and does not knowingly seek personal information from children in violation of applicable law. Discord's own age requirements and rules apply to use of the platform.

9. Security

Reasonable measures should be used to protect stored information. No online service can guarantee absolute security, and users should avoid submitting passwords, payment information, government identification, or other highly sensitive information to Mister Sinister.

10. Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated when Mister Sinister's data practices or features change. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when material changes are made.

11. Contact

For privacy questions, deletion requests, or concerns about Mister Sinister's handling of information, contact the bot owner using the support method listed above.
